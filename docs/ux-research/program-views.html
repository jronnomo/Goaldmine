Pixel mockup · 9 Aug 2026 · chosen direction: Marked Lane · all four panels must be inspected in both themes

Reading guide. Four 390px frames, left to right: unified Today, the /program dashboard, the cross-goal August calendar, and the SavedMeal quick-pick paired with a shared-metric chart. Identity is a monochrome geometric mark per member goal — ● Handstand, ■ Body comp, ▲ AWS — living in a fixed-width right-hand lane inside each row's 44px hit area. Hollow means claimed but not logged; solid means logged, and the link is written. Every item carries every claim it serves; nothing is suppressed for being someone else's rotation. Desaturate any screenshot here and no information is lost: hue is decoration, silhouette is identity. Dashed-left grey notes are annotation, never product UI.

⚠ Provisional / verify-visually

  1. CeilingRule stile overhang — 2px wide × 2px proud of the 6px bar top and bottom. Reads as a gate post at 390px; confirm it doesn't read as a rendering seam at 1×.
  2. CeilingRule hatch — repeating-linear-gradient(45deg, var(--border) 0 2px, transparent 2px 5px). In dark mode --border is #3A2E1F on --card #1A130C; the hatch may fall below the visible threshold.
  3. Warning-wash banner radius — 12px. The shipped class string (border border-l-[3px] p-4) specifies no radius.
  4. Sparkline y-domain — pinned 0–100 so the 80 ceiling stays on canvas. At day 15 every series hugs the floor. Alternative: auto-domain plus an out-of-range caret.
  5. Block-band Block 1 fill — 1 of 56 days ≈ 1.8%, a sub-2px sliver. Consider a 3px minimum so "started" is distinguishable from "not started".
  6. Mark lane width — 3 × 18px slots + 22px overflow + gaps = 82px. Confirm the longest row title still gets two lines at 390px rather than three.
  7. Mark glyph sizes — 14px in the Today lane, 13px in calendar cells (matching MarkerIcon size={13}), 16px in goal-card headers. Confirm ▲/△ optical weight against ●/○ across system font fallbacks.
  8. Positional vs packed lane slots — this mockup pins column 1 to Handstand so claims align as columns down the list. Packing left-to-right scans worse but scales past 3 goals. Playtest at 3 and at 5 goals.
  9. Fan-out stagger — 90ms between marks (0 / 90 / 180). The shipped .macro-flash is 270ms, so the last line finishes at 450ms.
  10. Observance em dash — — at 13px in var(--muted). Confirm it does not read as a minus sign or a redaction.
  11. Span-bar — 18px min-height, 12% muted wash. Confirm the label truncates gracefully on a 1-column continuation segment.
  12. +N overflow chip — 9px muted on var(--accent-soft), lifted from CalendarMonth.tsx. Verified small; re-verify beside three 13px marks.
  13. Gate-row Bullseye — size 14, hollow. At sizes below 14 the filled variant loses its centre ring, so gate rows must never drop below 14.
  14. BottomSheet scrim — color-mix(in srgb, var(--foreground) 45%, transparent) stands in for the literal rgba(0,0,0,0.45); the two diverge in dark mode.
  15. Fonts — this file uses system stacks for Geist / Geist Mono / DM Serif Display. Metrics, and therefore every wrap point above, will shift once the real faces load.
